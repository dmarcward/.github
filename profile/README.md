# DMARC Ward

> DMARC and email-security reporting.

DMARC Ward ingests standard email-authentication reports, turns them into clear dashboards, and helps teams watch alignment and protect their domains.

It started as a tool we ran for ourselves. It now serves organizations of any size that need continuous visibility into DMARC and related email-security reports — including domain management and straightforward plans as they grow.
